What your site is costing you — Luxury Homes of Las Vegas

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way a luxury buyer or seller would. Checked October 15, 2026

What I found

01

Your listings page is very slow in the load test, and your homepage is slow too.

In a simulated slow-connection load test, your All Listings page's first content appeared at 6.8 seconds and its largest part, a listing photo, at 18.3 seconds, with 860 milliseconds of blocked time. The biggest script on it is a listing-search file from idxhome.com, about 0.86 MB. Your homepage measured 2.4 and 4.3 seconds, and the page moved around as it loaded, with a layout-shift score of 0.193 (0.1 or under is rated good). The homepage is about 5.5 MB, and about 4.5 MB of that is the background video. One run each.

02

Your address differs between the page and its markup.

Your footer, Contact page and map link give 9081 W Sahara Ave #100, Las Vegas, NV 89117. The business markup on the homepage gives 6131 S Rainbow Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89118. The markup also has no hours and lists william@luxuryhomeslv.com as the email.

03

Template and placeholder pages are in your sitemap.

Your page sitemap lists “Communities template”, “Community page template”, “All Listings – Template”, “Sample Property Details”, “Testimonials template”, “Relocation template”, “Download Communities Images” and an agent page titled “Client Admin,”. They all load with a normal 200 response and a robots tag of “index, follow”. Your menu's Relocation link goes to /relocation-template/. 414 of the 475 addresses in that sitemap are automatic listing, open-home and market report pages, and the three I opened have no description.

04

Many pages share one description, and the homepage headings are internal labels.

13 of the 32 other pages I opened have the same description, “Luxury Homes of Las Vegas, established in 1999, is the leading real estate firm…”, including What Is My Home Worth, Exclusive Listings, Services and Getting Started. The two property pages I opened and the report pages have none. The homepage's only H1 is a hidden “Home”, and its section headings read “HP Slideshow”, “HP Proven Performance” and “HP Welcome”. The sales-numbers band reads “Over $0B” and “0+” in the page code until a visitor interacts.

05

Some details don't line up.

Bedroom counts on the homepage differ from your listings page for 5 of the 6 featured homes (43 Crested Cloud Way: 7 against 5, and its description says five bedrooms). The homepage says “trio” where the About page and the markup say “duo”.

06

Small fixes from my accessibility check.

It scored your homepage and your All Listings page 100. Reading the code, the blog thumbnails are all labeled “Blog”, the team photos “Agent” and 12 of 18 images on All Listings “Listings Address”. 29 links say only “Read More”, a missing icon file returns a 404 on every load, and 6 email links have no address in the page code.

What's working

Your office number and both owners' numbers are tap-to-call links on every page I opened, with the same numbers throughout. Your address, license numbers and social profiles are in the footer. The site has 193 community pages, 645 property pages, 561 blog posts and 9 team bios, with clear paths for buyers, sellers and relocation, and a home-value form. All 33 pages I opened load, and the main pages have their own title and description. In my searches, your own name brought up five of your pages, and your homepage was among the results for “luxury homes for sale Las Vegas”.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, how the site looks and loads on a phone (untested), whether your forms and email links work, and speed on pages other than the homepage and the All Listings page. I opened 33 pages plus 16 sitemaps, so most of your 645 property pages are unchecked. I didn't submit any forms or look at private data.
